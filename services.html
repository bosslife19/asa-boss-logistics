FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How fast can a rider get to me?

Riders are based in Yenagoa, so pickups usually start shortly after booking. Call or WhatsApp us and we will give you a time.

How much does a delivery cost?

Pricing depends on distance and item size. Send your pickup and drop-off locations and we will confirm the price upfront — no surprises.

Do you deliver outside Yenagoa?

We cover Yenagoa and nearby areas by bike. For other cities we handle the waybill — we take your package to the park and send it on the right transport line.

Can you collect payment from my customer?

Yes, we work with vendors on pay-on-delivery arrangements. Talk to us to set it up.

What are your opening hours?

We are open daily and take bookings until 7:00 pm.
